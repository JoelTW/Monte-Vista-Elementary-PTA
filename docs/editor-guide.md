# Editing the Monte Vista PTA website

A short guide for PTA volunteers who change the words and pictures on the website. You do not need to know anything about computer code. If you can fill in an online form, you can do this.

## What you need
- A free GitHub account (github.com). GitHub is where the website's files are kept.
- An invitation from the website admin. You will get an email from GitHub; click **Accept invitation**.
- A computer with Google Chrome is easiest.

## Signing in
1. Go to the editor address your admin gave you. It ends in **/admin/**.
2. Click **Sign In with GitHub** (or **Sign In Using Access Token** if your admin gave you one) and follow the prompts.

You will see a menu on the left. Each item opens a form with the text for one part of the website, and a preview of how it will look next to it.

## What is in the menu

**Pages** has one entry for each page of the website:

| Entry | The page it changes |
|---|---|
| Home page | The top banner, the President's message, the four action cards, the sponsors |
| About page, Events page, Get Involved page, Give page, Yearbook page, Contact page | The words, buttons and pictures on that page |
| Get to know your PTA | The board and chairs: names, roles, photos and short descriptions |

**System settings** holds things that apply to the whole site: the PTA email and address, the Join / Donate / Spirit Wear and social links (**Site settings**), and the tags and pictures on events (**Event categories**). Most editors will never need these.

## How a page is built: sections
Each page (except Home) is a stack of **sections**, from top to bottom. A section has one or two **columns** side by side, and each column holds **content blocks**: a heading, a paragraph, some buttons, a picture, a list, a form, and so on.

- To change words, open the page and click into the block.
- To add something, click **Add** under the column's content and pick what you want (for example **Paragraph** or **Buttons**).
- To add a whole new section, use the add button under **Sections of the page**. You can also copy a section that is close to what you want and change it.
- Drag blocks, columns and sections to put them in a new order, and use the bin icon to remove one.
- The preview on the right shows roughly how it will look. Forms, the calendar and the team grid show a note in the preview, and appear properly on the real site.

## Changing some words
1. Click the form, for example **Home page text**.
2. Click in the box you want to change and type. Each box has a plain label that says what it is. Watch the preview to see how it will look.
3. Click **Save**.

**Your change is a draft.** Saving does not change the live website. A draft waits until an approver publishes it, usually the website admin, and then the website updates by itself within a few minutes. Tell the approver when your draft is ready.

**Tip:** change one thing at a time so it is easy to check.

## Lists (people, cards, sponsors)
Some sections are lists, such as the board members or the sponsors. You can add a new item, change one, or remove one with the buttons beside the list. To change the order, drag an item to a new place.

## Pictures
- Use the picture box on the form to upload a new picture or pick one that is already there.
- **Only use pictures of children or families when the family has given permission.** The website is public, and anyone in the world can see the pictures.
- Always fill in the **Photo description** box. It is read aloud for people who cannot see the picture.
- Very large photos make the site slow. If you can, shrink photos to about 1,500 pixels wide before uploading.

## Events
Events are **not** changed in this editor. They come from the PTA's Google Calendar. Add or change the event in the calendar and it appears on the website within a few hours. The steps are in `docs/events-and-calendar.md`.

### The event tags
Each event gets a tag (for example "Fundraiser") and a picture, chosen by the words in the event's title. Open **Event categories** to see the words that go with each tag. An event gets the **first** tag in the list whose words appear in its title, so the order of the list matters.

## Things not to put on the website
The website is public, so never type or upload:
- passwords or bank details
- members' phone numbers, home addresses or payment information
- photos of a child without the family's permission

Names of board members and their PTA email are fine when the person has agreed.

## Seeing who changed what
Open the **history page**: the same address as the editor, but ending in **/admin/history.html** instead of **/admin/**. It lists every published change, newest first, with the person, the date and the change. It also lists **drafts waiting to be published**. Click a line to see exactly what changed on GitHub.

## If you make a mistake
Do not worry. Every published change is kept in the history with who made it and when, so nothing is lost. Tell the website admin what changed and roughly when, and they can put it back.

## Things the editor cannot do
Changing the menu, the colours, the layout or the form services is done by the website admin. Ask them.
